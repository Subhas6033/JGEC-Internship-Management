# JGEC Internship Management Portal — Documentation

The JGEC Internship Management Portal is a centralized web-based platform for Jalpaiguri Government Engineering College's Training and Placement Cell. It manages the complete internship lifecycle including online application submission, multi-level approval workflows (Student → TPO → SPOC), electronic No Objection Certificate (NOC) generation with Google Drive storage, and real-time email notifications for students, TPOs, SPOCs, and administrators.
